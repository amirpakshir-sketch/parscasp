Common questions

Where is my data stored? On your device only. See the privacy policy.

How do I add a cover photo to a report? Open the inspection, tap Create report, then Add cover photo.

How do I use AI suggestions? On supported devices the app uses on-device Apple Intelligence. You can also add your own Anthropic API key in Settings to use Claude. Always review AI text before saving.

Is the app a substitute for the code? No. PARS CASp is a field aid. Always verify requirements against the current CBC and ADA Standards and the authority having jurisdiction.

How do I change the language? Settings, then Language: English, Español or فارسی.
