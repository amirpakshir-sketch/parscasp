PARS CASp Privacy Policy

Effective November 2, 2026. Developer: Amir Pakshir.

PARS CASp is an accessibility inspection and CASp exam-prep app for California. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

No account, no tracking

The app has no sign-up and no login. It contains no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking. The developer does not collect, receive, sell or share your personal data.

Data stored on your device

Inspections, findings, measurements, notes, photos, signatures, inspector details and reports are stored only on your device. Reports you export or share leave the app only when you choose to share them, through the iOS share sheet.

Camera and photos

The camera and photo library are used only to attach photos you choose to findings, cover pages and signatures. Photos stay on your device.

AI features

On-device AI (Apple Intelligence) runs on your device, and nothing is sent to the developer.

Optional cloud AI (Claude by Anthropic) is off unless you enter your own Anthropic API key in Settings. The key is stored in your device Keychain. When you use this option, the question, the finding data and any photo you choose to analyze are sent directly from your device to Anthropic to produce the answer. That data is governed by Anthropic's privacy policy. It is never sent to the developer.

Children

The app is a professional tool and is not directed to children under 13.

Deleting your data

Delete an inspection in the app, or delete the app to remove all its data from your device.
